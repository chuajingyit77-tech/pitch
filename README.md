# Padang Besar Cross-Border Logistics Platform — Project Development Package

Investment-grade project development package for a proposed cross-border logistics platform at Padang Besar, Perlis, Malaysia — prepared for discussions with government agencies, investors, Shandong Hi-Speed Group, EPC contractors, technology partners and banks.

**Draft for discussion.** This package asserts no ownership, concession, operating rights or licences. All information is separated into four tiers throughout: publicly verified facts (with references), items pending confirmation from the project owner, proposed business models, and recommendations.

1. Executive Summary — why Padang Besar / Malaysia / border logistics; opportunities, challenges, the honest bottom line
2. Public Research — verified facts only, every statement referenced; source conflicts disclosed
3. Current Ecosystem — road, rail, warehouse, container, cold chain, border, customs flows
4. Project Opportunities — construction, operations, commercial, asset management, expansion, digital
5. EPC Work Packages — WP01–WP20 with packaging strategy
6. Digitalisation — 5-layer architecture, 16 capability modules, phasing, digital revenue logic
7. Prospek Cerah Business Model — why not a broker; eight lifecycle roles
8. Shandong Hi-Speed Role — verified profile, potential scope, three commercial postures
9. Investment Model — investors, revenue streams, capital structure, PPP options, risk allocation, exits
10. Financial Model Framework — assumptions structure only, deliberately no invented numbers
11. Due Diligence Checklist — 11 workstreams (corporate → digital)
12. Questions for Project Owner — 26 professional questions in 7 groups
13. Consortium Structure — SPV, equity, EPC, technology, operator, PMO, government, lenders
14. Risk Register — 25 risks, 10 categories, 5 gating risks
15. Roadmap — 7 phases, 5 governance gates, first-90-days plan
