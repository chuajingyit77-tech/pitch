## 13.3 Key agreements map

1. Shareholders' Agreement (governance, reserved matters, transfer restrictions, deadlock)
2. Land lease / sale & purchase / land-for-equity agreement — form depends on verified tenure
3. Concession / development agreement with state (if applicable)
4. EPC Contract (FIDIC Silver/Yellow basis typical for lender-financed EPC)
5. O&M / Terminal Operating Agreement(s)
6. Technology supply & integration agreements; SLAs for digital services
7. Development Management / PMO Agreement
8. Financing documents: facility, security, direct agreements (lender step-in)
9. Anchor customer / tenancy pre-commitments

## 13.4 Governance principles

- Board composition proportional to equity with protective minority rights
- Related-party contracts (Prospek services, SDHS EPC) disclosed, benchmarked, and approved by non-conflicted directors — essential for lender and co-investor confidence
- Independent lender's engineer and model auditor at financial close
- ESG and compliance committee from day one (border/customs environment demands clean governance)
