# Section 12 — Questions for the Project Owner

> Tone: collaborative, professional. These questions exist to help the owner assemble the evidence that investors, Shandong Hi-Speed, technology partners and banks will ask for anyway. Answering them early accelerates everyone.

## A. Project foundation
1. Could you walk us through the history of the project so far — how it originated, and what has been done to date?
2. What exactly does the project consist of in your current vision — site location, land area, facilities, phases?
3. What would success look like for you in 5 and in 15 years?

## B. Land & rights
4. Could you share the land titles or identify the parcels involved, so we can prepare the land review?
5. Is the land private, state, or reserved land — and what tenure arrangement do you envisage for the project company?
6. Are there any existing agreements over the land (options, leases, JV terms) we should be aware of?
7. Does any part of the concept rely on railway reserve or KTMB/Railway Assets Corporation land?

## C. Government & approvals
8. Which government stakeholders have you engaged so far, and is there anything in writing we could review (support letters, minutes, MOUs)?
9. Has the state government expressed a preferred structure (e.g., state participation, land lease, PPP)?
10. Have there been discussions with JKDM regarding bonded status, free zone status, or customs facilities at the site?
11. Has KTMB been approached about siding connection or rail services? What was the response?
12. Are you aware of any competing proposals for the same site or corridor?

## D. Commercial
13. Which cargo flows do you see as the anchor demand for the platform, and what is that view based on?
14. Are there prospective customers or tenants who have expressed interest — and may we speak with them at the right time?
15. How do you see this platform positioned relative to Perlis Inland Port and the existing Padang Besar terminal — integration, complement, or competition?
16. Are there Thai-side partners or counterparts in the picture?

## E. Structure & partners
17. What role do you wish to retain — landowner, shareholder, developer, or a combination?
18. What are your expectations regarding ownership share and control in a project company?
19. Are there existing commitments to any contractor, investor or advisor that a new consortium must respect?
20. How do you view foreign participation — both Chinese (e.g., Shandong Hi-Speed) and other international partners?

## F. Finances
21. What has been invested in the project to date, and by whom?
22. What is your expectation of the funding split between owner, partners and banks?
23. Are there liabilities attached to the project or the land that the consortium should understand early?

## G. Timeline & process
24. What timeline are you working toward, and are there external deadlines (political, land, funding) we should plan around?
25. Would you be open to a structured due diligence process under NDA, with a defined information list and data room?
26. Who on your side is authorised to negotiate, and how would you like decisions to flow between us?
