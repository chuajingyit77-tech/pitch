# Section 10 — Financial Model: Assumptions Framework

> **Status:** This section deliberately contains **no invented numbers**. It defines the structure of the financial model and every assumption that must be evidenced before figures are entered. Quantification requires the feasibility study, cargo demand study and confirmed land/rights position — all pending.

## 10.1 Modelling principles

1. Every input must trace to an evidence source: published statistics, signed term sheets, quotations, or owner-confirmed facts.
2. Three cases minimum (base / downside / upside) driven by cargo volume, tariff and ramp-up assumptions.
3. Model currency MYR; sensitivity to THB and USD/CNY for cross-border tariffs and imported equipment.
4. No revenue line may be included in the lender base case without an identified contractual or statistical basis.

## 10.2 Potential revenue sources (to be quantified only after evidence)

### Terminal & rail
- Rail container handling (lift-on/lift-off) per TEU
- Transshipment/interchange handling (road–rail; rail–rail interchange at the KTMB–SRT network boundary — note both networks are metre gauge, so this is an operational/commercial interchange, not a gauge break)
- Container storage (free-time then per-day), empty depot fees
- Reefer plug-in and monitoring
- Wagon/train handling and shunting service fees (structure depends on KTMB commercial arrangements — pending)

### Warehousing & property
- Ambient warehouse rent (per sq ft/month) — benchmark to northern-region industrial rents in feasibility
- Bonded warehouse premium rents and customs-service handling
- Cold chain: chamber rental, per-pallet storage, blast freezing, energy recharge
- Built-to-suit development returns; land sub-lease income (if tenure permits — pending)

### Border & vehicle services
- Truck marshalling / queue slot fees; overnight secure parking
- Weighbridge fees; inspection support services
- Driver amenities, fuel and F&B concessions

### Potential operating income (services)
- Freight handling labour services, VAS (labelling, kitting, palletisation)
- Customs brokerage facilitation (if licensed — licensing status pending)
- Equipment rental (forklifts, gensets, chassis)

### Potential recurring income
- Multi-year warehouse leases and anchor tenancy agreements
- O&M contracts on third-party facilities within the zone
- Utility resale margins (power, water, telecom) where regulations permit — regulatory check required

### Potential digital income
- Vehicle Booking System per-transaction fees
- Data subscriptions (border flow analytics) to shippers/forwarders
- Reefer/cold-chain monitoring-as-a-service
- Portal/EDI transaction fees; ICC monitoring services to zone tenants

### Potential PMO income (to Prospek/service entities, not SPV)
- Development management fee (milestone-based, pre-financial-close)
- PMO fee during EPC (typically % of capex or fixed monthly — to be benchmarked, not asserted)
- Technology integration management fee

### Potential asset management income
- Annual asset management fee (benchmark: % of asset value or fixed + KPI)
- Capex renewal planning and delivery fees
- Handback compliance management (if concession applies)

### Potential concession income (if a concession structure eventuates — pending)
- Availability-style payments for agency facilities (WP11), if government elects this route
- Free-zone licensing/administration income, if zone status is granted — **status pending confirmation**

## 10.3 Cost assumption categories (to be priced, not guessed)

- EPC capex per WP (Section 5) — from contractor pricing, not benchmarks alone
- Land cost/lease — from confirmed tenure terms (pending)
- Handling equipment capex and renewal cycles
- Energy (largest cold-chain opex driver), labour, maintenance, insurance, security
- Digital opex: licences, SOC, connectivity, refresh cycles
- Concession fees/royalties if applicable (pending)

## 10.4 Key model sensitivities to test

- Border throughput growth vs. historical rail/road volumes (Section 2 baseline)
- Ramp-up period length; anchor tenant pre-commitment share
- Cross-border rail interchange capture rate (depends on KTMB–SRT service patterns — evidence needed; note the standard-gauge break for China traffic sits in Laos, not at Padang Besar)
- Energy tariff escalation (cold chain exposure)
- MYR/THB movements on cross-border pricing competitiveness

## 10.5 Outputs the model must produce for each audience

- **Banks:** DSCR profile, gearing, tenor/tail, security package coverage
- **Investors:** equity IRR, payback, dividend profile, exit valuation logic
- **Government:** jobs, state revenue, trade facilitation KPIs, fiscal cost of any support
- **Board:** funding plan, milestone cash needs, downside survivability
