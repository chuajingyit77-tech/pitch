# Section 7 — Prospek Cerah: Business Model

> **Status:** Proposed business model, as articulated for discussion with partners. Prospek Cerah's current corporate standing, mandates, agreements and any rights relating to the project are **pending confirmation from project owner** and are covered by the due diligence checklist (Section 11).

## 7.1 Why Prospek Cerah is NOT a broker

A broker introduces parties, takes a fee, and exits — carrying no delivery obligation, no capital at risk, no ongoing accountability. The proposed Prospek Cerah model is the opposite on every dimension:

| Dimension | Broker | Prospek Cerah (proposed) |
|-----------|--------|--------------------------|
| Time horizon | Transaction close | Full project lifecycle (development → operations → expansion) |
| Accountability | None after introduction | Contracted deliverables: development milestones, PMO KPIs, digital SLAs |
| Capital | No skin in the game | Development capital at risk pre-financial-close; proposed SPV equity participation |
| Value creation | Access | Project definition, structuring, integration, delivery capability |
| Revenue | One-off commission | Development fees earned against milestones + long-term recurring income |
| Relationships | Introduces then exits | Holds and manages stakeholder relationships permanently |

The economic point for partners: Prospek's income is **earned against delivery**, not paid for access, and the majority of its proposed income arrives only if the project is built and operates well.

## 7.2 Prospek Cerah — eight roles

1. **Project Developer** — Originates and defines the project; funds/coordinates pre-development work (feasibility, master plan, approvals strategy); carries development risk to financial close; typical market practice is a development fee at financial close plus carried or paid equity.
2. **Consortium Builder** — Assembles and aligns the SPV shareholders, EPC, technology partners, operator and financiers; negotiates the shareholder, EPC, O&M and technology agreements as an integrated package rather than piecemeal deals.
3. **Business Development** — Builds the cargo book: pre-commitments from shippers, forwarders, hauliers, rail operators and Thai counterparts; anchor-tenant leasing for warehousing and cold chain; continuing commercial growth mandate during operations.
4. **PMO (Project Management Office)** — Owner's-side programme management across EPC packages WP01–WP20: schedule, cost, risk, interfaces, reporting to board/lenders; runs the CDE and reporting stack described in Section 6.
5. **Technology Integrator** — Accountable party for making WP15–WP17 systems work together and with government systems; manages the systems integrator(s) and acceptance testing.
6. **Digitalisation Partner** — Long-term operator of the digital layer (digital twin, ICC, data products) under SLA, converting Section 6 into recurring service income rather than a one-off IT project.
7. **Asset Management** — ISO 55000-aligned management of the built asset across its life: maintenance strategy, capex renewal planning, performance and handback compliance if a concession applies.
8. **Long-term Strategic Partner** — Standing counterpart to the project owner, state and federal stakeholders and to Shandong for expansion phases, adjacent opportunities and replication of the model at other border crossings.

## 7.3 How the roles fit the project lifecycle

- **Develop (Years 0–2):** roles 1–3 dominate — definition, consortium, cargo commitments.
- **Deliver (Years 2–5):** roles 4–5 dominate — PMO and technology integration.
- **Operate (Year 5+):** roles 6–8 dominate — digital services, asset management, growth.

## 7.4 Governance position

Prospek proposes to sit as (a) a shareholder in the SPV, (b) contracted PMO/development manager to the SPV, and (c) contracted digital & asset management services provider — with related-party contracts disclosed and benchmarked to market to satisfy co-investors and lenders. Exact shareholding and fee levels: to be negotiated; **no figures are asserted in this package**.
