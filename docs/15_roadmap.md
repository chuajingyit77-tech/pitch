# Section 15 — Development Roadmap

> Durations are indicative planning ranges typical for projects of this class, **not commitments**; the critical path depends on land verification and government processes that only the project owner can unlock.

## Phase 1 — Due Diligence (indicative 3–6 months)
- Execute NDA and information request (Section 11 checklist) with project owner
- Land & rights verification (gating item — nothing proceeds without it)
- Government soundings: state, NCIA, MOT, JKDM, KTMB
- Market scan: verified cargo baseline, PIP/TKPB competitive mapping
- Output: **Red Flag Report + Go/No-Go decision**

## Phase 2 — Consortium Formation (indicative 3–6 months, overlaps Phase 1)
- Heads of terms with project owner (roles, exclusivity, land pathway)
- Shandong Hi-Speed engagement: scope posture (Section 8), internal approvals started
- Operator and technology partner shortlisting
- Feasibility study + master plan commissioned; cargo demand study
- Output: **Consortium agreement / JDA signed; feasibility complete**

## Phase 3 — Investment Structuring (indicative 6–12 months)
- SPV incorporation; shareholders' agreement
- Land agreements executed (conditional); government support instruments negotiated
- Bankable feasibility finalised; financial model (Section 10) populated with evidence
- Lender market sounding; ECA/DFI processes; anchor customer pre-commitments
- Output: **Financial close**

## Phase 4 — Engineering (indicative 9–12 months, overlaps Phase 3)
- Master plan freeze; EIA and statutory approvals
- FEED/detailed design in BIM CDE; KTMB technical approval for sidings
- EPC tender/negotiation (WP01–WP17 packaging per Section 5); long-lead procurement
- Output: **EPC contract effective; notice to proceed**

## Phase 5 — Construction (indicative 24–36 months, phased)
- WP01–WP04 enabling works → WP05–WP10 core infrastructure → WP11–WP14 buildings
- Early-revenue sequencing: truck facilities and first warehouses ahead of full build
- PMO reporting to board/lenders; drone progress capture into digital twin
- Output: **Phased taking-over certificates**

## Phase 6 — Digitalisation (runs Phases 4–7)
- Phase A–D rollout per Section 6.4 (BIM/GIS from design; TOS/WMS/ANPR before opening; analytics and automation in operations)
- Output: **Integrated Command Centre live at opening day**

## Phase 7 — Operations & Growth (Year 1+ after opening)
- Ramp-up management; anchor tenants onboarded; service KPIs tracked
- Asset management regime (ISO 55000) in force; refinancing evaluation after stabilisation
- Phase 2 expansion triggered by utilisation thresholds (WP20)
- Output: **Stabilised operations; expansion decision; exit options open (Section 9.6)**

## Governance gates

| Gate | Decision | Evidence required |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| G1 | Proceed past DD | Clean land/rights, government support signals, demand baseline |
| G2 | Sign consortium | Feasibility positive, partners aligned, heads of terms with owner |
| G3 | Financial close | Bankable package: contracts, approvals, funding committed |
| G4 | Notice to proceed | EPC signed, land access, insurance, conditions precedent met |
| G5 | Open for operations | Commissioning complete, licences in force, operator ready |
