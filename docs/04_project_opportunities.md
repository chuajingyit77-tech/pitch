# Section 4 — Project Opportunities

> Opportunity map across the platform lifecycle. Items marked ◆ depend on land/rights confirmation; items marked ● depend on government/agency agreement. All are proposals, not existing mandates.

## 4.1 Construction opportunities

| Opportunity | Notes |
|-------------|-------|
| Site development & earthworks ◆ | Platform formation in the Padang Besar/Chuping corridor; flood-resilient design essential |
| Warehouse construction ◆ | Ambient, bonded, cold chain; steel-frame fast-track builds; smart-warehouse spec (SDHS–PTT Synergy precedent shows the model is active in Malaysia) |
| Rail works ◆● | Sidings, terminal trackworks, possible future transshipment/interchange tracks — all subject to KTMB agreement |
| Road works ◆ | Internal networks, border-approach improvements offered to JKR, truck marshalling infrastructure |
| Utilities ◆ | Substations, water, fire systems, solar PV, standby power — cold chain drives heavy power demand |
| Buildings ◆● | Admin, commercial, agency/customs facilities, workshops, driver amenities |
| Digital infrastructure ◆ | Fibre, data centre, ICC, gates — built with civil works, not retrofitted |

## 4.2 Operations opportunities

- Terminal operations (rail yard + container yard) — self-perform or operator partner
- Warehouse operations: 3PL contracts, bonded operations, cold-chain management
- Truck terminal operations: marshalling, VBS slots, secure parking
- Facility management: platform-wide FM, security, landscaping
- Equipment services: maintenance, rental, PTI for reefers

## 4.3 Commercial opportunities

- Anchor leasing to 3PLs, forwarders, Thai and Malaysian shippers, e-commerce players
- Free-zone VAS: labelling, kitting, halal certification support, light assembly ●
- Trade services cluster: brokers, banks, insurance, surveyors as tenants
- Fuel/retail/F&B concessions serving driver traffic
- Advertising and event/exhibition uses at a strategic border showcase site

## 4.4 Asset management opportunities

- ISO 55000 asset management regime as a paid service to the SPV (and third parties in the zone)
- Energy management: solar PPA, energy-as-a-service for cold-chain tenants
- Capex renewal programme management across a 30+ year life
- Digital twin–based lifecycle optimisation (Section 6) — data-driven maintenance economics

## 4.5 Expansion opportunities (Phase 2+)

- E-commerce fulfilment campus riding cross-border e-commerce growth
- Halal logistics hub aligned with CVIA's halal-industry positioning ●
- Cold-chain corridor scaling with Thai perishables trade
- Standard-gauge readiness if national/Pan-Asian plans extend south ●
- Replication: apply the platform + digital model at other Malaysian border crossings (Rantau Panjang, Bukit Kayu Hitam) — Prospek/consortium IP play
- Border Economic Zone participation once incentives are gazetted ●

## 4.6 Digital opportunities (summary — full strategy in Section 6)

- Corridor visibility platform (booking → gate → customs status → delivery)
- Data products for shippers, forwarders and agencies
- Smart-warehouse services layered on SDHS-ecosystem automation capability
- Command-centre services sold to zone tenants and, potentially, agencies ●
