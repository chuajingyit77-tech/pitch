    The marginal-cost argument

    A competitor selling you drone inspection alone must charge you the full cost of the flight. Once that same flight already exists for A1 building inspection, the marginal cost of also producing A7 collateral revaluation, B1 progress verification and B3 collateral monitoring is close to zero. The 13th module costs a fraction of the 1st. That is why the programme is bought as a base, not as a shelf of tools — and why buying it piecemeal from three vendors costs more and delivers less.
